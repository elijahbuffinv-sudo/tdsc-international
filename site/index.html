        Historical and Corporate Disclosure

        Thiel Detective Service Company (TDSC) traces its historical origins to 1873. The original business was subsequently dissolved, and the present-day TDSC brand was later adopted and reintroduced by Delator Group. The current organization has not operated continuously since 1873, and references to 1873 describe the origin of the name, not the age of the present company. TDSC is a subsidiary and DBA of Delator Group and operates as part of the Delator Group corporate organization. Read the TDSC history · Visit Delator Group

        TDSC operates from Nashville, Tennessee and maintains no offices outside the United States. International engagements are scoped from headquarters and, where local work requires it, coordinated with appropriately licensed local professionals.
